The Problem

Wellness apps are generic. Stress is personal.

One-size-fits-all content

Headspace and Calm serve the same library to everyone. Your Tuesday after a sleepless night looks identical to your best Sunday morning.

No real companion

Existing apps are content players, not coaches. When you're anxious at 2 AM, a playlist doesn't talk back. There's no one to guide you through it.

Western lens on Eastern practice

Meditation apps strip out the source — pranayama, mantra, and Vedic philosophy — and repackage a diluted version. Authenticity is the gap.

970 million people worldwide live with a mental health condition — and the #1 reason people abandon wellness apps is that the content doesn't fit how they actually feel.

The Solution

A wellness app that listens first, then guides.

1 · Morning Reflection

Every day starts with a 20-second check-in: mood (8 states), energy, and sleep quality. This becomes the signal that drives everything else.

2 · Mood-Rule Engine

A smart recommendation engine maps your state to a personalized morning → afternoon → evening routine. Poor sleep + low energy? It escalates to stress-relief protocols automatically.

3 · AI Companion — 5 personalities

Pranayama Guru for breath work · Gita Companion for life wisdom · Sleep Guide for wind-down · Confidence Coach for self-doubt · Relationship Guru for love, family & connection. Voice in, voice out — a real conversation.

4 · Authentic content library

Guided breathing (4-7-8, Box, Alternate Nostril), meditations, Himalayan & temple soundscapes, mantras, and a TikTok-style reels feed of wisdom — built to make ancient practice a daily habit.

Unique Selling Point

The only wellness app where AI meets authentic yogic tradition

Personalization competitors can't match

Calm and Headspace were built pre-AI: static libraries, manual browsing. Karmana was born AI-native — every session is chosen for your mood, energy, and sleep that day. Retrofitting this onto a 10-year-old content catalog is hard. Building on it from day one is our moat.

A companion, not a catalog

Users don't search for content — they talk to a guru. Voice-first AI conversation with distinct personalities creates an emotional bond no playlist can. This is the difference between a tool and a relationship.

Authentic Indian wellness heritage

Pranayama, mantra, Gita philosophy, Himalayan soundscapes — from the source, not sanitized. This resonates with 1.4B Indians, the global diaspora, and a Western audience increasingly seeking authenticity over corporate mindfulness.

Whole-person loop

Check-in → recommendation → practice → streak → reflection. A closed feedback loop that gets smarter with every day of use — data flywheel from day one.
