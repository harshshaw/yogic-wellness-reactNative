How it works

Your day, beautifully structured

Karmana fits naturally into your morning and evening routine — taking just 10 minutes to set the tone for your entire day.

1

Reflect on how you feel

Answer a short, guided morning check-in. Rate your energy, mood, sleep quality, and focus. Your AI companion reads between the lines.

2

Breathe with intention

Choose a pranayama practice that matches your energy. The visual breath guide paces you through each cycle effortlessly.

3

Rest and restore

Wind down with nature sounds or sleep music. Let your nervous system shift from doing to being — anytime you need it.

4

Watch yourself grow

Your streak, scores, and insights compound over time. In 30 days, you'll see the data of a changed life.
